# ☕ Samad's Coffee Station — Telegram Bot Marketing Plan

An end-to-end data-driven marketing plan and product growth strategy aimed at scaling the Telegram pre-ordering bot for **Samad’s Coffee Station** at Inha University in Tashkent (IUT) from **200 to 500 transactions/month**.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

> **Course:** Marketing Management Team Project (Group 22)  
> **Institution:** Inha University in Tashkent  
> **Business Goal:** Achieve 500 pre-ordered coffee transactions per month through the Telegram bot.

Samad’s Coffee Station faced heavy morning break congestion and low bot awareness (60% of students didn't know it existed). Based on primary research (99 survey responses & in-depth interviews), our team designed a targeted marketing strategy focusing on convenience seekers, physical QR placement, and operational UX improvements.

---

## 🔬 Research & Analytics Highlights

* **Sample Size:** 99 student & staff survey respondents + 3 student interviews + 1 owner interview.
* **Key Finding 1 (Peak Congestion):** 75% of purchases happen between 8:00 AM – 12:00 PM; 66.3% face severe overcrowding during breaks.
* **Key Finding 2 (Awareness Gap):** 59.6% were unaware of the Telegram bot, and only ~10% of aware users had ever used it.
* **Key Finding 3 (Motivation):** 67.8% indicated discounts/loyalty rewards and 66.7% cited line-skipping as top adoption drivers.

---

## 🎯 Marketing Strategy & STP

* **Target Segment:** **Convenience Seekers** — time-sensitive students buying coffee during peak morning hours[cite: 2].
* **Positioning:** Specialty handcrafted coffee with speed, convenience, and a personal touch[cite: 2].
* **Marketing Mix (4Ps):**
  * **Product:** Core quality coffee + low-cost snacks/cookies & bug-free bot[cite: 2].
  * **Price:** Value-based pricing with digital incentives (*"Every 6th cup free"*)[cite: 2].
  * **Place:** Express **Bot Pickup Shelf** at the station to bypass main queues[cite: 2].
  * **Promotion:** QR-code stickers in campus elevators, A3 posters, and merch stickers for active users[cite: 2].
